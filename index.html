Meet Bayou

Get lost in the good stuff

Bayou is a native Bluesky app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac that makes it easy to catch up, read whole conversations, and follow the rabbit hole wherever it goes.

Coming soon to the App Store
Your timeline

Catch up on what’s happening right now

Open Bayou and you’re at the top of now. Your Following timeline, custom feeds, and lists sit side by side, a swipe apart.

Conversations

Read the whole conversation

Threads show their shape, with replies nested under the posts they answer. Follow the branch that interests you without losing your place.

Curators

Follow what interesting people like

Anyone’s public likes can be a feed of their own. Pin the people whose taste you trust and see what they’re finding, with nothing ranked by an algorithm you can’t see.

Bayou for Mac

Follow the rabbit hole, in as many windows as you like

A real Mac app, not a stretched phone. Give feeds, likes, searches, and chats their own windows, and move through all of it from the keyboard.

  • Windows for everything

    Open anything pinned in your sidebar, from a feed to someone’s likes to a chat, in a window of its own.

  • Keyboard first

    Arrow through posts, jump between sections with ⌘1 through ⌘9, and start a post with ⌘N.

  • A sidebar that’s yours

    Pin feeds, people’s likes and media, searches, and conversations.

Everything else

All of Bluesky, done natively

  • Custom feeds and lists

    Find feeds for whatever you’re into, pin your favorites, and build lists of people worth following.

  • Every profile, explorable

    Posts, replies, media, videos, likes, feeds, lists, and starter packs, a tap apart.

  • Messages

    Chat one on one or in groups, react to messages, and share posts straight into a conversation.

  • Notifications you choose

    Turn push notifications on or off for each kind, from replies and mentions to new followers.

  • Alt text, with a hand

    Describe images yourself, or have Apple Intelligence draft a description for you to edit.

  • Your moderation

    Muted words, the labelers you subscribe to, and per-label controls that match Bluesky’s own.

  • Threads and drafts

    Write a whole thread at once, save drafts, add GIFs, and choose who can reply.

  • Video on your terms

    Videos play when you tap them, never on their own.

  • All your accounts

    Sign in to as many Bluesky accounts as you have and switch between them instantly.

No ads. No tracking.

Bayou talks to Bluesky directly from your device. There’s no Bayou account and no Bayou server holding your posts. The only thing we run is the gateway that delivers push notifications, and only if you turn them on.
